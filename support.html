Support

A person reads every message

Small app, small team. There's no ticket system and no bot — write to us and you'll get a human, usually within a couple of days.

Email us

hello@getdonny.com — for anything at all: a bug, a wrong date, a story you want us to cover, or a question about your data. There's also a feedback form in the app, under the You tab, which reaches the same inbox.

01 Something in a card is wrong

Tell us, and please say which card. This is the most useful message you can send us — the whole point of Factbox is being right about things and showing our working, so a correction is a gift rather than a complaint.

Every card cites at least two independent sources, and you can read them by tapping the book icon at the bottom of any card. If our sources disagree with yours, send yours and we'll look at both.

02 I lost my progress

Factbox has no account and no server. Everything — which stories you've read, how far you got, your streak — lives on your device only.

That means deleting the app deletes your progress, and there is no way for us to restore it. Nor does it move to a new phone unless it comes across in an encrypted device backup. We know that's a real limitation; it's the price of collecting nothing about you, and it's the first thing we'll change when we add accounts.

03 Notifications aren't arriving

Factbox sends one local reminder a day, at a time you choose, and nothing else. It's scheduled on your phone rather than pushed from a server.

If it isn't arriving: open iOS Settings → Factbox → Notifications and check they're allowed. Also check Focus modes and Scheduled Summary, both of which will silently hold a notification back. If they're on in iOS and still not appearing, write to us with your iOS version.

04 I want to suggest a story

Please do. The catalogue is written by hand, so suggestions genuinely shape what gets made next. Tell us the subject and, if you have one, the thing about it that surprised you — that's usually where a good card starts.

05 Am I being charged for this?

No. Factbox is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising. If you see something claiming otherwise, it isn't us — tell us and we'll look into it.

06 What do you know about me?

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. We don't know who you are or what you've read. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what that means and where the few exceptions sit — chiefly that if you email us, we obviously then have your email address.

07 Accessibility

Factbox supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion — with Reduce Motion on, the animated scenes hold a single frame instead of looping. If something is hard to use with an assistive technology, tell us what and on which screen. We'd rather fix it than have you work around it.
