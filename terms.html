02 · What Factbox is

A history reader for a phone. Season one is fifty-one stories, told across 450 cards, each card set on a painting or a photograph from Wikimedia Commons. A story runs about a minute and a half to two and a half minutes, and can carry a quiet ambient sound bed, which is off unless you turn it on.

It is general-interest reading. It is not a textbook, not a qualification, not academic advice, and not a substitute for reading the sources themselves.

05 · Access, and the restore link

There is no server-side account and no password. Signing up means this browser remembers your email address and your preferences; nothing is registered with us, and logging in on a second phone therefore cannot bring your access with it. Access lives in the browser you bought it in. To carry it to another phone or another browser, you use the restore link the site shows you after you subscribe.

That link is a bearer token: whoever holds it has access. Keep it to yourself. Publishing it, or handing it around so that people who have not subscribed can read the season, is a breach of these terms, and it is the one thing here we would actually mind.

Clearing your browser's site data erases your access along with everything else the site stored — so save the restore link somewhere before you do that. Your reading progress and your saved stories are stored the same way, and cannot be recovered by us, because we never had them.

06 · Getting the history right

The stories are written by hand and checked before they ship. Where a card rests on a specific document, a named authority or a disputed reading, the card says so. Not every card carries a citation, and we do not claim that every one does. Where historians genuinely do not know, the card says that rather than picking a confident answer.

That is a standard we hold ourselves to, not a guarantee we can give. The site is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. History is argued about, sources conflict, and we will occasionally be wrong. If you find an error, write to hello@getdonny.com with the story and the card; corrections go in as soon as we have checked them. A correction is a gift, not a complaint.
