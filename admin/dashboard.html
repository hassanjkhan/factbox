The journey

/firststory to paid, and back again

One funnel, end to end: arriving at /firststory, reading into the story, reaching the end card where the sign-up ask is, reaching the sign-up page, signing in, being sent to Stripe, paying, and coming back on a later day. Every number here is about the people who were on that page in this window — not the site as a whole — and each step shows what share of the step above it reached.

Subscribers

the number that is true

Read straight from Firestore rather than from a browser event, which is why this is the count that wins when it disagrees with the journey funnel above. Someone can pay on a phone and come back on a laptop: the funnel's last step misses them and this does not.
