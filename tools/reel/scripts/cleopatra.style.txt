STYLE — appended verbatim to every prompt in this reel, never paraphrased.

Hand-drawn 2D editorial illustration, textured brushwork, muted palette, like a
premium illustrated history magazine made for social media. Historically
accurate first-century BC Egyptian and Roman clothing, architecture and
objects. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not Pixar, not anime, no modern objects.
Artwork bleeds off all four edges of the canvas, no frame, no border, no
margin, no cream edge. ABSOLUTELY NO text, NO captions, NO lettering or
writing anywhere in the image.

CLEOPATRA APPEARANCE LOCK — the same woman in every image she appears in: a
young Ptolemaic Greek-Egyptian woman of about twenty-one, olive complexion,
strong straight nose, dark intelligent eyes, dark hair worn in a simple
Hellenistic style or under a plain diadem. She wears fine draped Greek-style
linen, occasionally with Egyptian jewellery. She is NEVER a modern beauty, is
NEVER shown with a heavy black wig and dramatic winged eyeliner, and is the
SAME woman in every image. In any image where she appears, EXACTLY ONE woman
is Cleopatra; every other woman must look clearly different in face, hair and
dress.

CAESAR APPEARANCE LOCK — the same man in every image he appears in: a Roman
man in his fifties, lean angular clean-shaven face, high forehead with
receding grey hair cropped short, sharp deep-set eyes, a white toga with a
purple border. He NEVER has a beard, NEVER wears armour in an indoor scene,
and is the SAME man in every image. In any image where he appears, EXACTLY ONE
man is Caesar; every other man must look clearly different in face, hair and
dress.

Composition: named foreground action, specific middle-ground activity,
background depth. Vertical 9:16 framing, the subject placed for a phone
screen.
