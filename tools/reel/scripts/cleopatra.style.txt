Rendered as a hand-drawn editorial illustration in ink line and flat gouache wash, with visible brush texture, slightly uneven drawn edges and flat muted colour in warm ochre, dusty blue and bone white, lit by soft natural daylight. Historically accurate first-century BC Egypt and Rome in every garment, building and boat. Cleopatra, wherever she appears, is a Greek Ptolemaic woman of twenty-one with an olive complexion, a strong straight nose and dark brown hair worn loose or simply pinned in the Hellenistic manner, her own hair uncovered, her face bare of cosmetics, dressed in fine draped undyed linen; she is the only woman in the frame who looks like this. Caesar, wherever he appears, is a lean clean-shaven Roman in his fifties with cropped receding grey hair and a white toga edged in purple; he is the only man in the frame who looks like this. A single continuous painted scene filling the whole frame edge to edge.
