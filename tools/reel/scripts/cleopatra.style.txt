First-century BC Egypt and Rome. Cleopatra, wherever she appears, is a Greek Ptolemaic woman of twenty-one with an olive complexion, a strong straight nose and dark brown hair worn loose or simply pinned in the Hellenistic manner, her own hair uncovered, her face bare of cosmetics, dressed in fine draped undyed linen with an ochre wrap; she is the only woman in the frame who looks like this, and never wears a straight-cut black wig, beaded braids or heavy black eyeliner. Caesar, wherever he appears, is a lean clean-shaven Roman in his fifties with cropped receding grey hair and a white toga edged in purple; he is the only man in the frame who looks like this.
